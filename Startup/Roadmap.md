## Connectivity through Mesh Network

To achieve a system that is both off grid and low power, a mesh network can be used to "hop" the messages across distances. This allows festivals with poor cellphone coverage to stay connected and Larvae to keep there Consortium in order even without a 5G/LTE signal.
